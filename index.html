Why try an arbitrage bot?

Arbitrage means catching the same asset at two different prices across venues or routes — then letting software move fast while you stay in control.

Two prices, one desk

Markets do not sync perfectly. A pair can quote differently on two venues or Jupiter routes. Plumb watches those gaps, surfaces them on your board, and can act when your rules pass — on a self-hosted desk you run.
