Why a desk bot

Same asset. Two prices. One machine watching both.

Books do not sync. The gap opens for seconds and closes before a human finishes reading it. Software is the only thing fast enough to be there — so you set the rules and let it hold the watch.

Two prices, one desk

Markets do not sync perfectly. A pair can quote differently on two venues or Jupiter routes. Plumb watches those gaps, surfaces them on your board, and evaluates them against your rules — on a self-hosted desk you run in dry/HOLD until you choose to arm.

Speed, eyes, your keys

You cannot watch every book by hand. A desk bot brings speed and constant eyes; you bring the rules, the size, and the choice to stay dry or arm when ready. Keys stay with you. Control stays in a private chat on your phone — no browser dashboard required.

Custody & boundaries

Everything stays on your side of the line.

Exact boundaries: what we hold, what we never hold, what we can see, and what we cannot.

What you hold

Your wallet keys, your funded wallet, your VPS or host, your RPC credentials, the bot token for your control chat, and the running binary after handoff. You host it; you arm it; you accept the risk.

What we never hold

We do not custody funds, do not take a cut of fills, and have no access to your balance at any point. No pooled capital, no managed account, nothing to deposit with us.

What we can see

What you put in the brief, what you share on the handover call, and support messages you send us. During build we configure the binary for your named pairs — we do not run it on our infrastructure.

What we cannot see

Your private keys, your wallet balance, your actual fills, or your RPC traffic after handoff. We are not in the signing path unless you explicitly share logs for support.

Control from your phone

Start, pause, size, and read status from a private, encrypted chat on your phone. No browser dashboard required — the desk talks to you where you already are.

Before you buy

The questions operators actually ask.

Straight answers, including the ones that cost us sales.

Do you ever hold my keys, my wallet, or my funds?
No — never, on any seat. Plumb runs on your machine with your keys and your wallet. We do not custody funds, we do not take a cut of fills, and we have no access to your balance at any point. If anyone selling desk software asks you to deposit with them, walk away.
What exactly do I get after I pay?
A ready bot built for the pairs you name in the brief, delivered within 5–7 days of confirmed payment and brief, plus a handover call to get it running on your host. Starter and Pro ship the running binary; Source ships the source code as well. Every seat is controlled from a private chat on your phone — no browser dashboard to keep open. Ships dry/HOLD by default.
Will this make me money?
We will not promise that, and you should distrust anyone who does. Plumb is a tool: it gives you speed and constant eyes on the book, but the rules, the size, and the risk are yours. Markets can move against you and you can lose money. Start small, run dry first, and treat early sessions as training.
What do I need on my side to run it?
A machine that stays online (a small VPS is plenty), a Solana RPC endpoint, a bot token for your private control chat, and a funded wallet you control. We walk through every one of these on the handover call — you do not need to have them ready before you pay.
Which seat should I start on?
If this is your first desk bot, take Starter and learn the surfaces on a partial board. If you already trade actively and want the multi-pair arbitrage board with your own slippage and tip settings, take Pro — that is the seat most operators end up on. Take Source only if you intend to edit the code yourself.
What happens when the update window ends?
Nothing breaks. Your seat is a lifetime licence and your desk keeps running exactly as it is. Updates are included for 3 months on Starter and 6 months on Pro and Source; after that, continued updates and support are an optional $149/mo. You are never forced onto a subscription to keep what you bought.
Is this a token, a fund, or an investment?
None of those. You are buying a piece of software, once, and running it yourself. There is no token, no pooled capital, no managed account, and nothing to deposit with us.
