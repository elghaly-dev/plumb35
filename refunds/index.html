Refunds

If you want your money back, you get your money back

No window. No conditions. No form. Write one line to support@elghaly.dev and we send it back.

The whole policy

Ask for a refund and you get a refund. Before the brief, after the brief, before delivery, after delivery, a week later, a month later. It does not matter which.

You do not have to give a reason, and we will not ask you for one. You do not have to prove anything, show anything, or answer any questions. There is no form to fill in and nobody to convince.

You get back what you paid. Not a part of it. We cover any network fee on the way back, so the number that left your account is the number that returns to it.

Why it is written this way. The other kind of refund policy exists so that a company can argue with someone about their own money. We would rather lose a sale than win that argument. If Plumb is not what you wanted, the money was never really ours.

How to ask

Email support@elghaly.dev with the word refund. Include the address or card you paid from if you have it to hand — it makes it faster, and if you do not have it we will find it.

We reply to say it is done. If you have not heard back within 24 hours, write again — that means the first mail did not reach us, not that the answer was no.

How it reaches you

Card, via Stripe. Sent back to the same card. Stripe hands it to your bank the same day; your bank decides when it lands, usually five to ten working days. That part is out of our hands and we will not pretend otherwise.

Wallet transfer. Sent back to the wallet it came from, unless you tell us to use a different one. One mechanical limit, and it is the only one on this page: we cannot refund to an exchange deposit address, because those addresses are not yours and the funds can be lost. Give us a wallet you hold the keys to.

What we keep

Nothing.

What a refund does not do

It does not reach into your machine. Plumb is self-hosted — after delivery the files are on your side, and we have no way to take them back and no intention of trying. So a refund is us returning your money and trusting you to stop using the software. That trust is deliberate. It is the same trust we ask you for when we say the desk ships dry and holds.

Refunding does not delete anything you built with it, and it does not stop you buying a seat again later.

What this page is not

This is a refund policy, not a guarantee about the software. Plumb is sold as-is. Getting your money back is unconditional; the desk earning money never was and is not promised anywhere. See Limits for what we do not claim.
