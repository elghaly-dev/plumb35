Limits

What we cannot show you

Read this before you pay. If you need proof we cannot provide, do not buy on hope.

  • Your fills, your balance, your RPC traffic After handoff we are not in the signing path and cannot see your private keys, wallet balance, actual fills, or RPC traffic unless you explicitly share logs for support (see Custody · c4). We cannot show a stranger your desk operating on their capital before purchase.
  • Results We cannot show results, and we do not claim any. Plumb is desk software sold as-is. Markets can move against you; you can lose money. Start small, run dry first.
  • Screenshots are not proof A screenshot is an image we produced. Only a transaction signature — copyable, checkable on two independent explorers — is on-chain evidence. That is why the On-chain page lists three signatures with Solscan links instead of screenshots — and why it shows the borrow size only, with no result figures.
  • Fixtures prove layout, not behaviour Static SVG fixtures — not a running desk, not proof of fills. SHA-256 confirms file bytes, not that the desk behaves as the picture suggests. See Fixtures.
  • Nothing here predicts what your desk will earn No statement on the sales page or proof pages is a forecast, return, CLEAR claim, or land claim. Board screenshots are UI reference only.
  • No landed arbitrage claim We have never claimed a landed arbitrage on this page, and we are not claiming one now.
  • If you need proof of profitability before you buy We do not have it. Do not buy on hope.

What you can check alone in under two minutes: open the Glance SVG fixtures and match their SHA-256 hashes (Fixtures); read which sales-page statements are verifiable vs our word (Claims).
