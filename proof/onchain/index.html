A buyer can verify on-chain activity with a transaction signature pasted into independent explorers. We have no publishable signature yet, so this page shows no on-chain example and no figures. Do not buy based on screenshots.

What a real on-chain artifact would show

When a signature is published, a stranger can paste it into two independent explorers and read the same instruction path. That would show a flash borrow and repay path executing on chain.

It would not show results, not a fill at any particular price, and not a claim about what your desk will earn. A screenshot alone proves none of that — only the signature does.
